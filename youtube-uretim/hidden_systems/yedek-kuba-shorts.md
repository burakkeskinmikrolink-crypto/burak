## Short 1: "Bir ülkeyi nasıl yeniden başlatırsın?"
- **Süre:** ~40 sn
- **Hook:** HOW DO YOU RESTART A COUNTRY?

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | NASA Black Marble: Küba kararıyor | THE WHOLE GRID IS DEAD |
| 4–12 sn | Termik santral (Wikimedia Commons) | A POWER STATION NEEDS POWER TO START |
| 12–24 sn | Animasyon: dizel jeneratör → tek santral → küçük ışık adası | BLACK START: ONE ISLAND AT A TIME |
| 24–34 sn | Adalar birleşiyor | SYNC · JOIN · REPEAT |
| 34–40 sn | Bir ada tekrar kararıyor | ONE WOBBLE → START AGAIN |

**VO (tam metin):**
> How do you switch a whole country back on?
> A big power station needs electricity just to start: pumps, fans, controls. If the grid is dead, there's nothing to start it with.
> So engineers do a "black start." A diesel generator wakes one station. That station powers a small island of the grid, often around hospitals. Then islands are synchronised and joined, one by one.
> In Cuba this year, that process has had to happen again and again, and sometimes the grid fell over before it finished.

**Açıklama:** Black start: çökmüş bir şebeke nasıl yeniden başlatılır? Küba'nın 2026 karartmaları üzerinden.

**Kaynak:** UPI, CNN (UNE açıklaması), NESO (black start tanımı)

## Short 2: "Bir yılda kaç kez?" (sayaç)
- **Süre:** ~30 sn
- **Hook:** 7 TIMES IN ONE YEAR?

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Sayaç "1… 2… 3…" | NATIONWIDE GRID COLLAPSES, 2026 |
| 4–14 sn | Takvim: Mart, Temmuz (3 kez/9 gün), Ağustos, Eylül işaretleniyor | 3 IN 9 DAYS (JULY) |
| 14–22 sn | "21 hours" saat ikonu | AUGUST: COLLAPSED 21 HOURS AFTER RESTORATION |
| 22–30 sn | "7" büyük, altında "by UPI's count" | SEPTEMBER: NO. 7 (UPI) |

**VO (tam metin):**
> How many times can a country's entire power grid collapse in one year?
> In Cuba in 2026, the count depends on who's counting. But by UPI's count, the collapse in mid-September was the seventh.
> In July, the grid failed three times in nine days. In August, it went down again just twenty-one hours after power had been restored.
> Fuel shortages, ageing plants and almost no spare margin. The full story is on Hidden Systems.

**Açıklama:** Küba'nın 2026'daki ulusal şebeke çöküşleri. Sayı kaynaklara göre değişiyor, bu Short UPI'nin sayımını kullanıyor.
**Hashtag:** #cuba #blackout #energycrisis #powergrid #hiddensystems

## Short 3: "İngiltere'de olsa?" (UK karşılaştırması)
- **Süre:** ~35 sn
- **Hook:** COULD BRITAIN GO DARK LIKE THIS?

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | İngiltere yüksek gerilim direkleri (Wikimedia Commons) | COULD BRITAIN GO DARK? |
| 4–12 sn | Haritada Küba ada, çevresinde bağlantı yok | CUBA: AN ISLAND GRID, NO NEIGHBOURS |
| 12–26 sn | "60% in 24h · 100% in 5 days" metin grafiği | UK RESTORATION STANDARD |
| 26–35 sn | NOC ekranı, batarya göstergesi | A PLAN FOR THE DAY IT ALL STOPS |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Could Britain's grid go completely dark like Cuba's? A total blackout is possible anywhere. The difference is the recovery.
> Cuba is an island grid. It can't borrow power from its neighbours, and it's short of fuel.
> Britain plans for the worst. Under the Electricity System Restoration Standard, by the end of 2026 the system operator must be able to restore sixty percent of demand in every region within twenty-four hours, and all of it within five days.
> A grid needs a plan for the day it stops.

**Açıklama:** İngiltere'nin elektrik geri getirme standardı ve Küba'nın ada şebekesi arasındaki fark.
**Hashtag:** #uk #powercut #neso #cuba #hiddensystems
**Kaynak:** GOV.UK Electricity System Restoration Standard, NESO
