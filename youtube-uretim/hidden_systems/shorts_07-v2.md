## Short 1: Gizli süper güç (~36 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Karanlık bir evde eski kablolu telefon çalıyor, ahize kalkıyor (Pixabay) | THE LIGHTS WENT OUT. IT WORKED. |
| 2–14 sn | Animasyon: santral → bakır kablo → telefon, akım noktaları akıyor | POWER CAME DOWN THE WIRE |
| 14–26 sn | Router, fişi prizde; fiş çekilince ışıklar sönüyor | DIGITAL: NEEDS YOUR POWER |
| 26–36 sn | "31 JAN 2027" | COPPER SWITCHES OFF |

**VO:**
> The lights went out. It worked.
> That was the old landline's hidden superpower. Its electricity came down the copper wire from the telephone exchange, not from your plug.
> Digital landlines run through your broadband router, and the router needs mains power. Without a battery backup, a power cut can silence them. On the thirty-first of January 2027, Britain's copper network switches off. So remember the old promise, while it lasts:

**Döngü:** "…while it lasts:" → "The lights went out. It worked."

**VO:**
> One hour. That's the rule.
> In Britain, phone providers must make sure you can reach emergency services for at least an hour in a power cut, with a battery backup or a mobile phone.
> If you don't have a reliable mobile signal, Ofcom says your provider must offer a backup solution for free. Critics say an hour is too short. So if the power goes tonight, how long have you got?
