## Short 1: "60 saniyede sistem" (animasyonlu anlatım)
- **Süre:** ~40 sn
- **Hook:** THE INVOICE MACHINE

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 sn | El çizimi animasyon: fabrika → mağaza | FACTORY SHIPS PARTS |
| 5–12 sn | Takvim, "90 DAYS" | SHOP PAYS IN 90 DAYS |
| 12–20 sn | Fatura → finansçı, "$97k now" | SELL THE INVOICE FOR CASH TODAY |
| 20–30 sn | Para okları: mağaza → fabrika → finansçı; son ok kesiliyor | THE MONEY PASSES THROUGH THE FACTORY |
| 30–40 sn | Kırmızı "$9B+" | WEEKS LATER: BANKRUPTCY |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Here's a hidden system most people never think about.
> A factory ships parts to a shop. The shop pays in ninety days. The factory doesn't want to wait, so it sells that invoice to a lender for cash today. Normal. Legal. Everywhere.
> But often, the shop keeps paying the factory, and the factory forwards the money to the lender.
> Now imagine the factory stops forwarding it. That's what a Jefferies-affiliated fund said happened at First Brands, weeks before a bankruptcy with more than nine billion dollars in liabilities.
> Full story on Hidden Systems.

**Açıklama:** Factoring nasıl çalışır ve bir şirket ödemeleri aktarmayı bıraktığında ne olur? First Brands çöküşü.
**Hashtag:** #firstbrands #factoring #bankruptcy #finance #hiddensystems
**Kaynak:** Banking Dive (Jefferies/Point Bonita), Octus

## Short 2: "Rakam karşılaştırma"

- **Hook:** $9,000,000,000 vs $12,000,000

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Bölünmüş ekran: sol kırmızı, sağ gri | OWED vs IN THE BANK |
| 4–10 sn | Sol: "$9B+" büyüyor | LIABILITIES: $9B+ |
| 10–15 sn | Sağ: "$12M" küçük | CASH: ~$12M |
| 15–25 sn | Fatura üç kopyaya ayrılıyor | ALLEGED: FAKE INVOICES · DOUBLE-PLEDGED COLLATERAL |
| 25–30 sn | Terazi ikonu | NOT GUILTY PLEA · EX-CFO PLEADED GUILTY |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Left: what First Brands owed when it filed for bankruptcy. Over nine billion dollars.
> Right: the cash in its corporate bank accounts. About twelve million.
> How do you get there? Prosecutors allege fake invoices and collateral pledged to more than one lender. The founder and his brother pleaded not guilty. The former finance chief pleaded guilty.
> And in August 2026, a judge sent the company into liquidation. The criminal trial is still to come, so for now, these remain allegations.

**Açıklama:** First Brands'in iflas başvurusundaki rakamlar. Ceza davası sürüyor, iddialar kanıtlanmadı.

**VO (tam metin):**
> British viewers, this will feel familiar.
> In 2021, Greensill Capital collapsed in London, and Credit Suisse froze around ten billion dollars of funds tied to it.
> In 2025, a US auto-parts company called First Brands collapsed, leaving funds linked to Jefferies and UBS exposed.
> Different details, same hidden system: finance built on invoices that investors rarely see behind.
> I work in network operations. We call that a monitoring gap: everyone watching their own slice, nobody watching the whole.
