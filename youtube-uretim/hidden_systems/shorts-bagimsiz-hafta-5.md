| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |

| 12–24 sn | Teksas haritası, mahalleler sırayla kararıyor | MILLIONS CUT TO SAVE THE GRID |

**VO:**
> 4 minutes and 37 seconds.
> February 2021. A winter storm knocked out power plants across Texas, and the grid's frequency fell toward a danger line. If it stayed below 59.4 hertz for nine minutes, protection systems would trip generators and could bring the whole grid down. Operators cut power to millions of homes to stop the fall. The grid operator later said it came within 4 minutes and 37 seconds of that point. The blackouts were painful. A full collapse could have taken weeks to recover from. All decided in…

**Döngü:** "…All decided in…" → "4 minutes and 37 seconds."
**Kaynak:** ERCOT board meeting, 24 Feb 2021, reported by KUT/Texas Standard ("4 minutes and 37 seconds away from collapsing")

Tiny, but it added up. Grid operators fixed the shortfall, then ran the grid slightly fast so the clocks could catch up. That's how, in 2018…
