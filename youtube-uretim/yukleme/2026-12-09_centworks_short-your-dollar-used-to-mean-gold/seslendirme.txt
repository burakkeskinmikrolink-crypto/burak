Your dollar used to mean gold. [pause 400]
Until 1971, foreign governments could swap U.S. dollars for gold at 35 dollars an ounce. That August, President Nixon suspended it. The Federal Reserve's own history site describes the move as a response to a looming run on U.S. gold and to rising inflation at home. Within two years, the world shifted to floating exchange rates. Since then, the dollar's value rests on the economy behind it, not metal in a vault. Strange to think that…
