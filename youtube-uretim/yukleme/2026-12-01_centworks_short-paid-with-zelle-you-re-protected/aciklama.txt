Paid with Zelle? You're protected?
For buying from strangers, often not. Zelle moves money from one bank account to another in minutes. If you approved the payment yourself, federal rules generally don't require your bank to return it, even if the item never arrives. Zelle itself says to only send money to people you know and trust.
