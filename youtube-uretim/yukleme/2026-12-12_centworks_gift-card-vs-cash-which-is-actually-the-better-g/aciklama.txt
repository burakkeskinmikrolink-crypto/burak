Americans are holding about $27 billion in unused gift cards and store credit. So this holiday season: gift card or cash? Five rounds — value, lost balances, the federal rules on expiration and fees, risks like card draining, and how gifts actually feel — plus the one question that decides it.

This channel is made by an engineer, not a banker. Educational content, not financial advice.
