Check the box on your statement.
Every credit card statement has a warning box. By law, it shows how long it would take to pay off your balance if you only made minimum payments, and how much you'd pay in total. It also shows what you'd need to pay each month to clear it in three years. Those numbers assume you buy nothing new.
