Every credit card statement has a warning box. By law, it shows how long it would take to pay off your balance if you only made minimum payments, and how much you'd pay in total. It also shows what you'd need to pay each month to clear it in three years. Those numbers assume you buy nothing new. Most people never read it, but it might be the most honest number your card company sends. So tonight, open the app and…
