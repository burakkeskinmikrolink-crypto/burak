Every disaster has lined-up holes.
In 2000, the psychologist James Reason described accidents with a simple picture: slices of Swiss cheese. Each slice is a defence: a backup, an alarm, a checklist, a person. Every slice has holes, and the holes keep moving. Most days, one slice catches what another misses. A disaster happens when, for one moment, the holes in every slice line up.
