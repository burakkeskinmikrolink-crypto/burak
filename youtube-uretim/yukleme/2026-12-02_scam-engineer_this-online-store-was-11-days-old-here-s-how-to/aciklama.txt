The store looked perfect: photos, five-star reviews, a padlock in the address bar. It was eleven days old, and the boots never arrived. Here's, calmly, how fake online shops get built in an afternoon, why social media ads lead to them, what the padlock really means — and three quick checks to use before your next holiday order.
