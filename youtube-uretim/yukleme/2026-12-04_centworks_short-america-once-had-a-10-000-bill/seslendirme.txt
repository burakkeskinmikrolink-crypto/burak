America once had a $10,000 bill. [pause 400]
There were also five hundred, one thousand, and five thousand dollar notes. They were last printed in 1945. In 1969, the Treasury and the Federal Reserve announced they'd be discontinued because hardly anyone used them. They're still legal tender, but collectors pay far more than face value.
