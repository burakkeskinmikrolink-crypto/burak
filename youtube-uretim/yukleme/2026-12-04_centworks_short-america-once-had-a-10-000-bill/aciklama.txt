America once had a $10,000 bill.
There were also five hundred, one thousand, and five thousand dollar notes. They were last printed in 1945. In 1969, the Treasury and the Federal Reserve announced they'd be discontinued because hardly anyone used them. They're still legal tender, but collectors pay far more than face value. Since then, the biggest bill being printed has been the hundred. Hard to believe today, but…
