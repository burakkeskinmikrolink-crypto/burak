A US auto-parts giant owed more than $9 billion — and had about $12 million in the bank when it filed for bankruptcy. This is the hidden system of invoice finance, told as a timeline, and how it can fail silently.

All criminal allegations described in this video are allegations; Patrick James and Edward James have pleaded not guilty and are presumed innocent.

This channel is made by someone who works inside a mobile network operations center.
