Hike, hold, or cut?
If the Fed hikes, credit card rates rise within a billing cycle or two. If it holds, high-yield savings keep paying more, if you shop around. If it cuts, borrowing gets cheaper, slowly. And your fixed-rate mortgage? It doesn't move in any branch. In every branch, one move helps: paying down variable-rate debt. Because nobody knows which way it goes next. Hike…
