Same price. Less in the bag.
That's shrinkflation, and the government's inflation numbers do count it. The Bureau of Labor Statistics converts prices to a price per unit, like per ounce. So when a bag shrinks from 16 ounces to 14 at the same price, the inflation index records a price increase. It isn't always easy to catch.
