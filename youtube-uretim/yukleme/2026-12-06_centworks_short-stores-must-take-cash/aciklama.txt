Stores must take cash?
Not under federal law. The Federal Reserve says a private business can set its own payment policy, unless a state or local law says otherwise. And some do. Massachusetts, New Jersey, Rhode Island and Colorado have rules, and so do New York City, San Francisco, Philadelphia and D.C. Everywhere else, card only is legal.
