How do you restart a country? [pause 400]
If Britain's entire grid went dark, you couldn't just switch it back on. Most power stations need electricity to start. So the system operator keeps a plan, once called black start, now called restoration. A few special plants that can start on their own come up first. They power a small piece of the network, connect some homes, and build a stable island. Then islands join up, one by one. Engineers rehearse it, hoping never to do it. Because someone has to know: how do you restart…
