Because a grid has to match supply and demand every second. When demand outruns supply, frequency falls, and power stations start disconnecting to protect themselves, which makes the shortfall worse. That's how a shortage becomes a collapse. So operators cut some customers, in planned blocks, and rotate them, so the whole system survives.
