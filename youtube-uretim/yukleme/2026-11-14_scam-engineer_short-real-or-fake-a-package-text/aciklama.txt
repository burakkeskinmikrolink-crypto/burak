Real or fake? A package text.
"Your package is on hold. Update your address here," with a link. Fake. The Postal Inspection Service says USPS won't text you a link unless you signed up for tracking on that package. Second: a tracking update you asked for, with nothing to pay. Can be real. Third: a tiny redelivery fee, by link. Fake. Not sure? Type the website yourself and enter the tracking number. Next text you get, ask: real or fake…

Source: U.S. Postal Inspection Service – Smishing; FTC – New FTC data show top text message scams of 2024
