A great price, but the owner is overseas and wants a deposit before you see it. Fake. The F T C lists both as red flags. Second: a landlord who shows you inside and gives you a written lease. More likely real. Third: pay the deposit by gift card, wire, or crypto. Fake. Search the address online. If the same home is listed elsewhere with a different contact or price, walk away.
