More than $10,000 in cash?
When someone deposits or withdraws more than ten thousand dollars in cash in a day, the bank files a Currency Transaction Report with FinCEN, the Treasury's financial crimes office. That's routine. It isn't an accusation. What is illegal is splitting cash into smaller amounts on purpose to avoid the report. That's called structuring, and it can be a crime even if the money is perfectly clean.
