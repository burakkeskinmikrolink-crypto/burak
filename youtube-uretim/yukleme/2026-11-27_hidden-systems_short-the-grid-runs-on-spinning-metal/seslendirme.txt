The grid runs on spinning metal. [pause 400]
Big power-station generators are heavy, and they spin in step with the grid. If one power station suddenly trips, all that spinning mass keeps turning for a few seconds, slowing how fast the frequency falls. nee-so calls this inertia, and it buys the control room time.
