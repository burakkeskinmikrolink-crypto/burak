The grid runs on spinning metal.
Big power-station generators are heavy, and they spin in step with the grid. If one power station suddenly trips, all that spinning mass keeps turning for a few seconds, slowing how fast the frequency falls. NESO calls this inertia, and it buys the control room time. Wind and solar connect through electronics, so they don't provide it naturally. That's why Britain now runs machines that generate nothing at all. They just spin, because…
