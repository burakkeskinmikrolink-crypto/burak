Inside a mountain in North Wales sits Dinorwig, a pumped-storage power station. When electricity is cheap, it pumps water up to a lake at the top. When demand suddenly spikes, the water falls back down through turbines. Its operator says it can reach maximum output, around 1,700 megawatts, in under 16 seconds.
