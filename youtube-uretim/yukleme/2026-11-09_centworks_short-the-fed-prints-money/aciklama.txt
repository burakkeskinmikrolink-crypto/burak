The Fed prints money?
Not physically. Paper dollars are printed by the Bureau of Engraving and Printing, part of the Treasury. Coins come from the U.S. Mint. The Federal Reserve places an order for new notes each year and puts them into circulation through banks. And most money isn't paper at all. It's numbers in bank accounts, created when banks make loans. So the next time someone tells you…
