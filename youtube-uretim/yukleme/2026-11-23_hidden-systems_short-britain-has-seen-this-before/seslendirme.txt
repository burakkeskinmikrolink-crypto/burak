Britain has seen this before. [pause 400]
In 2021, GREEN-sill Capital collapsed in London, and Credit Suisse froze around ten billion dollars of funds tied to it.
In 2025, a US auto-parts company, First Brands, collapsed, leaving funds linked to Jefferies and UBS exposed.
Different details, same hidden system: finance built on invoices that investors rarely see behind. Every lender watched its own slice. Nobody watched the whole. And if that sounds familiar…
