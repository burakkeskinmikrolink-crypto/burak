Real or fake? A suspicious order.
A call says an expensive phone was ordered on your Amazon account. Press one to cancel. Fake. The FTC says scammers pose as Amazon this way, then ask for remote access or gift cards to give you a "refund." Second: an order you don't recognize, inside your own Amazon app. Real, and you can handle it right there. Third: "We refunded too much, send the difference back." Fake. Big shopping week, more fake calls. So, real or fake…
