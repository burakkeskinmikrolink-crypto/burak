The internet lives under the sea. [pause 400]
When you video-call someone abroad, your data very likely crosses a fibre-optic cable lying on the seabed. In early 2025, the research firm TeleGeography counted around 570 submarine cable systems in service, with more than 80 planned. Each one comes ashore at a small number of buildings on the coast, and specialist ships stand by to repair them when they break. Wi-Fi feels like it's in the air.
