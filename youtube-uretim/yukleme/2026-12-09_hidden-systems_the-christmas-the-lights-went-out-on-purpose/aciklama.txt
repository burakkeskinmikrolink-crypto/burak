On Christmas Eve 2022, power companies in the eastern United States switched off electricity to their own customers — on purpose. Here's the hidden rule every power grid lives by, why 90,500 megawatts of generation vanished in the cold, how rotating outages work, and whether the same thing could happen in Britain this winter.

This channel is made by someone who works inside a mobile network operations center.
