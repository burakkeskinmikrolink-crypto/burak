Big tax refund: a bonus?
Not exactly. A refund is your own money coming back, money you overpaid during the year. By May 2026, the IRS reported an average refund of about 3,276 dollars. That's roughly 270 dollars a month you lent the government, at zero interest. Some people like it as forced savings, and that's a fair choice.
