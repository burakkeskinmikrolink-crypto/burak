About one in seven payments in America is still made in cash — and for millions of people, it's almost all of them. What if cash disappeared? Who wins, who gets left out, whether a store can legally refuse cash, and what Sweden learned about keeping a backup.

This channel is made by an engineer, not a banker. Educational content, not financial advice.
