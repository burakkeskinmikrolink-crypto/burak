9 August 2019. Lightning hit a transmission line north of London. That alone is routine; the grid is built for it. But within moments, an offshore wind farm and a gas power station both lost output, each linked to the strike. Frequency fell so far that automatic protection disconnected more than a million customers to protect everyone else. Some trains stopped.
