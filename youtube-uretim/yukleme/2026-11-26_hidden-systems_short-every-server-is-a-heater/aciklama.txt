Every server is a heater.
Almost all the electricity a server uses turns into heat. So a data centre is really a giant heater that happens to do maths. When cooling fails, servers have one safe move: switch themselves off. That's what happened in an Amazon data hall in Virginia in May 2026, and Coinbase's core trading was disrupted for hours.
