For a hundred years, the landline kept working when the power went out — because its electricity came down the copper wire from the exchange. On 31 January 2027, Britain's copper phone network switches off. Here's the hidden system that's disappearing, what replaces it, what Ofcom requires in a power cut, why telecare users are most at risk, and the four things to check at home. (The US is making the same move: AT&T plans to retire its copper network by the end of 2029.)

This channel is made by someone who works inside a mobile network operations center.
