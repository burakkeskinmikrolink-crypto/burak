One substation fire closed Heathrow.
Late on 20 March 2025, a fire broke out at North Hyde substation in west London. Heathrow lost power and closed for most of the next day. The national system operator's review found the most likely cause was moisture getting into a transformer component. An oil test had flagged high moisture back in 2018, and it was never fixed.
