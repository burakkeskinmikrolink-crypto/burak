August 2003, Ohio. In a utility control room, the software that should have warned operators about problems stopped working, and nobody realised. Power lines sagged into trees and tripped, one after another, while the screens looked calm. Within hours, a cascade cut power to about 50 million people across the northeastern United States and Ontario.
