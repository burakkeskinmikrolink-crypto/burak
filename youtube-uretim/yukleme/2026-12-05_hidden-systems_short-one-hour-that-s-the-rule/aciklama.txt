One hour. That's the rule.
In Britain, phone providers must make sure you can reach emergency services for at least an hour in a power cut, with a battery backup or a mobile phone. If you don't have a reliable mobile signal, Ofcom says your provider must offer a backup solution for free. Critics say an hour is too short. So if the power goes tonight, how long have you got?
