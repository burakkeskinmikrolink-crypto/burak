The Fed sets mortgage rates?
Not directly. The Fed sets one short-term rate: what banks charge each other overnight. Mortgage rates mostly follow long-term bond yields, which move on what investors expect over years. When markets expected more hikes this fall, Freddie Mac's thirty-year average reached seven point four percent. The Fed sets the mood, not your rate. So next time someone tells you…
