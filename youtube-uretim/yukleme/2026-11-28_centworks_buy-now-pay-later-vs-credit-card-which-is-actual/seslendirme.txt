Two numbers, measuring two different things.

In December 2025, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau published a report on pay-in-four loans from six big providers, using 2023 data. That year, 53.6 million Americans took out at least one of these loans. The share of loans hit with a late fee was 4.1 percent — down from 5.2 percent the year before.
