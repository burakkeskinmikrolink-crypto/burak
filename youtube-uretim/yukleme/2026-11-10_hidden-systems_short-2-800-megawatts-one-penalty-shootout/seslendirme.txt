2,800 megawatts. One penalty shootout. [pause 400]
July 1990: England against West Germany. When the shootout ended, millions of people did the same thing at the same moment. They put the kettle on. National Grid recorded a surge of about 2,800 megawatts, a figure it still calls its all-time record. Control rooms plan for this. They study the TV schedule and keep fast power ready, like hydro stations that start in seconds. Because the grid still remembers…
