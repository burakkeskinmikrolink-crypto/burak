July 2012, India. On the 30th, the northern grid collapsed. The next day, three regional grids failed in a cascade, and reports put the number of people without power at more than 600 million. India's power minister blamed states drawing more than their allocated share; an expert committee later called for better monitoring and communication between grid operators.
