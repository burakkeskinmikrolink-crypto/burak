The $2 bill never went away.
It's still legal tender, and the Federal Reserve still orders new ones in some years. In other years, it orders none at all, because there are already enough in storage. Most people just don't ask for them, so banks rarely hand them out, so people rarely see them, and the cycle repeats. Any store that takes cash should take a two. It's real money. It's just shy.
