$2.99 or $3.00?
One cent apart. But many shoppers judge the first as noticeably cheaper. Researchers call it the left-digit effect: we read prices left to right, and the first digit drops from three to two. A 2005 study in the Journal of Consumer Research found the effect is strongest exactly when that left digit changes. That's why so many Black Friday tags end in nine.
