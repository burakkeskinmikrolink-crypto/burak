Stolen card: credit or debit?
It matters. With a credit card, federal law caps your liability for unauthorized charges at fifty dollars, and many cards offer zero. With a debit card, the cap depends on speed. Report within two business days: up to fifty. After that: up to five hundred. Wait more than 60 days after your statement: you could lose everything taken.
