Why doesn't the Fed target zero? [pause 400]
Since 2012, the Federal Reserve has aimed for 2% inflation over the longer run. Not zero. A little inflation gives the economy a buffer against deflation, when prices fall and people delay spending, which can feed on itself. It also leaves the Fed more room to cut interest rates in a downturn. And official measures may overstate inflation slightly. Two percent is a target, not a promise.
