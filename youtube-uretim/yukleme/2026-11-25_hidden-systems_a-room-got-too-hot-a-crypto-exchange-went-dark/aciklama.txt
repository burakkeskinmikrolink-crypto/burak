In May 2026, cooling failed in an Amazon Web Services data hall in Northern Virginia. Servers shut themselves down to survive the heat — and Coinbase's core trading was disrupted for hours. Here's the hidden system of data-centre cooling, why recovery takes so long, and why Coinbase rebuilt around three zones instead of two.

This channel is made by someone who works inside a mobile network operations center.
