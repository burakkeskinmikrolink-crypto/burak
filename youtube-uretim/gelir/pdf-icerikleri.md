## 1) Scam Engineer: "The Scam Check Card" (buzdolabı kartı)

**Dosya adı:** `scam-check-card.pdf` · **Bağlı videolar:** EV-01, SE-03, SE-04, SE-05, EV-04

```
THE SCAM CHECK CARD
Keep this by the phone.

THE 4 SIGNS
1. SURPRISE — You weren't expecting this call, text or email.
2. HURRY — "Today", "right now", "or your account will be closed".
3. A PATH — They give you a link, a number to call back, or an app to install.
4. AN ASK — Money, a code, a password, gift cards, crypto, or remote access.

Two or more signs? Stop. Don't use their path.

THE ONE RULE
Hang up. Then contact the company yourself, using the number on your card,
your statement, or the official app you already have.
A real bank, Medicare or government agency will never be upset that you checked.

NEVER SHARE
• One-time codes (the 6-digit texts) — with anyone who contacted you
• Your Medicare or Social Security number — with an unexpected caller
• Remote access to your computer — with someone who called or popped up

PAYING? REAL ORGANIZATIONS DON'T ASK FOR
gift cards · cryptocurrency · wire transfers to "keep your money safe" · cash handed to a courier

IF SOMETHING HAPPENED
1. Call your bank using the number on your card. Ask to stop or reverse the payment.
2. Report it: ReportFraud.ftc.gov  ·  ic3.gov (FBI)
3. Identity stolen? IdentityTheft.gov
4. Scam text? Forward it to 7726 (SPAM), then delete it.
5. Age 60+? National Elder Fraud Hotline: 1-833-FRAUD-11 (1-833-372-8311)

Anyone who asks for a fee to "recover" your money is running a second scam.

My bank's number: ______________________
☐ Our family has a code word for emergency calls. (Don't write it here.)

The Scam Engineer · youtube.com/@thescamengineer
Educational information, not legal or financial advice. Updated: [AY YIL]
```

## 2) Centworks: "Before You Swipe" kontrol listesi

**Dosya adı:** `before-you-swipe.pdf` · **Bağlı videolar:** CW-04, CW-05, CW-06, CW-07

```
BEFORE YOU SWIPE
Five questions that decide what a purchase really costs.

1. WILL I PAY THE FULL BALANCE THIS MONTH?
   Yes → a rewards card can put some of the hidden swipe fee back in your pocket.
   No  → the interest will usually cost more than any points you earn.

2. PAY IN 4 OR CREDIT CARD?
   Can I make all four payments on time, from money I already have?
   Yes → "0% interest" can be cheaper.
   No  → late fees and missed payments can cost more than the card.
   Count every plan you already have open. Several small plans add up.

3. IS MY DEBT FIXED OR VARIABLE?
   Fixed (most mortgages) → rate changes don't touch your payment.
   Variable (credit cards, some loans) → pays to shrink it first, whatever the Fed does.

4. IS MY SAVINGS EARNING ITS KEEP?
   Compare your savings rate with high-yield accounts at insured banks.

5. DO I HAVE A LITTLE CASH AT HOME?
   Card networks can go down. Emergency checklists say: keep some cash.

If a store refuses cash: it's legal in most places — unless your state or city
has a cash law. Look it up once.

Centworks · youtube.com/@centworks
Educational information, not financial advice. Numbers change — check current rates.
Updated: [AY YIL]
```

**Kaynak eşlemesi:** Soru 1 → CW-04 (Fed araştırması). Soru 2 → CW-06 (CFPB, anket verileri). Soru 3–4 → CW-05. Soru 5 ve nakit yasası → CW-07 (Ready.gov, Fed SSS).
