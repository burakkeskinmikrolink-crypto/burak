## Short 13 — 1 Ara | QUIZ: Giving Tuesday bağışı (~35 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Bir kalp simgesi telefondan çıkıp bir bağış kutusuna düşüyor | GIVING TUESDAY. WHO GETS YOUR GIFT? |
| 2–12 sn | Şıklar | A) GIFT CARD B) CARD BY PHONE C) LOOK IT UP |
| 12–15 sn | Sayaç | 3… 2… 1… |
| 15–35 sn | "C" yeşil; resmi web sitesi + "charity watchdog" büyüteç | C) GIVE ON THE OFFICIAL SITE |

**VO:**
> Giving Tuesday. Who gets your gift?
> A caller asks you to help families this winter. How should you give? A: a gift card, right now. B: your card number, on this call. C: hang up, look the charity up yourself, and give on its official website. It's C. The FTC says never give to anyone who insists on cash, gift cards, wire transfers, or crypto. Check the charity with a watchdog first. Generosity is a good thing. Just make sure you know:

**Döngü:** "…make sure you know:" → "Giving Tuesday. Who gets your gift?"

## Short 15 — 4 Ara | GERÇEK Mİ SAHTE Mİ?: Banka dolandırıcılık SMS'i (~34 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Üç uyarı bildirimi sırayla düşüyor (kurgusal banka adı) | REAL OR FAKE? BANK FRAUD TEXT. |
| 2–14 sn | "Did you attempt a $500 payment? Reply YES or NO." → hemen ardından gelen arama | FAKE |
| 14–22 sn | Bankanın kendi uygulamasındaki uyarı | MORE LIKELY REAL |
| 22–34 sn | "We'll text you a code. Read it to me." | FAKE |

**VO:**
> Real or fake? Bank fraud text.
> "Did you attempt a 500 dollar payment? Reply YES or NO." You reply NO, and the "fraud department" calls right away. Fake. Replying told them a real person was reading, and the call is the actual scam. Second: an alert inside your bank's own app. More likely real. Third: "We'll text you a code. Read it to me." Fake. Your bank's fraud team won't need your one-time code. Next alert? Real or fake…

**Döngü:** "…Real or fake…" → "Real or fake? Bank fraud text."
**Kaynak:** FTC – New FTC data show top text message scams of 2024 (fake bank fraud alerts among top types); FTC – How to recognize and avoid phishing scams

C: a request to deposit your own money, often crypto, to unlock more tasks. It's C. The FTC says these task scams exploded in 2024, and reported job-scam losses topped 220 million dollars in just six months. Real jobs never make you pay to get paid. So:
