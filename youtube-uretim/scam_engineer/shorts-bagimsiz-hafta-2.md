## Short 5 — 17 Kas | KISA HİKÂYE: Jüri görevi cezası (~36 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Telefon ekranında kurgusal "County Court" araması; bir tokmak simgesi iniyor. "Composite character" | YOU MISSED JURY DUTY. PAY NOW. |
| 2–14 sn | "Pay today with gift cards or a warrant will be issued." | A REAL JUDGE'S NAME. A BADGE NUMBER. |
| 14–26 sn | Ron telefonu kapatıp mahkeme numarasını kendisi arıyor (eller, yüz yok) | HE CALLED THE CLERK HIMSELF |
| 26–36 sn | Zarf simgesi "Summons by mail" | COURTS DON'T COLLECT BY PHONE |

**VO:**
> You missed jury duty. Pay now.
> That's what the caller told Ron. He used a real judge's name and a badge number. The fine could be cleared today, with gift cards, or there would be a warrant. Ron hung up and called the court clerk's office himself. There was no warrant. Federal courts say they send jury summonses by mail and never ask for money or sensitive information by phone. A fine can only come from a judge, in court. So if a caller ever says:

**Döngü:** "…a caller ever says:" → "You missed jury duty. Pay now."
**Kaynak:** U.S. Courts – Scams target citizens with false jury service claims (2017); U.S. District Court S.D. Georgia – Warning: jury scams

## Short 6 — 18 Kas | GERÇEK Mİ SAHTE Mİ?: Askıya alınan SSN (~33 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Üç konuşma balonu sırayla açılıyor; ilki kurgusal bir robot sesi dalgası | REAL OR FAKE? YOUR SSN, SUSPENDED. |
| 2–12 sn | "Your Social Security number has been suspended. Press 1." | FAKE |
| 12–20 sn | Posta kutusunda resmi zarf (kurgusal) | CAN BE REAL |
| 20–33 sn | Ekranda "Social Security Admin." yazan arama | CALLER ID PROVES NOTHING |

**VO:**
> Real or fake? Your SSN, suspended.
> A recorded call says your Social Security number has been suspended. Press one. Fake. Social Security says it will never call to tell you your number is suspended. Second: a letter from Social Security about your benefits. Can be real. Third: caller ID that says Social Security Administration. Proves nothing; caller ID can be faked. Unsure? Use the number on ssa.gov, and report impostors at oig.ssa.gov. Next call? Real or fake…

**Döngü:** "…Real or fake…" → "Real or fake? Your SSN, suspended."
**Kaynak:** SSA Office of the Inspector General – Scam awareness; U.S. Attorney's Office releases citing SSA warnings

## Short 7 — 20 Kas | QUIZ: Parkmetre QR kodu (~33 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Parkmetre çizimi; üstüne ikinci bir QR etiketi yapıştırılıyor | SCAN THE PARKING METER CODE? |
| 2–12 sn | Şıklar | A) YES B) IF IT LOOKS NEW C) CHECK FIRST |
| 12–15 sn | Sayaç | 3… 2… 1… |
| 15–33 sn | "C" yeşil; tırnağın etiketi kaldırdığı yakın plan | C) STICKER ON TOP? WALK AWAY |

**VO:**
> Scan the parking meter code?
> A: yes, it's right there on the meter. B: only if the sticker looks new. C: check first. Is the code part of the meter, or a sticker on top of it? It's C. The FTC warns that scammers cover real parking-meter codes with their own, leading to fake payment pages. Pay through the city's official app, or at the meter itself. And skip codes that arrive in unexpected texts. So next time you park, before you…

**Döngü:** "…before you…" → "Scan the parking meter code?"
**Kaynak:** FTC – Scammers hide harmful links in QR codes to steal your information (Dec 2023); CBS Miami – Fort Lauderdale warns about fake QR code stickers

## Short 8 — 22 Kas | KISA HİKÂYE: Yanlış numara (~36 sn)

|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Bir SMS balonu beliriyor: "Hi Susan, still on for golf?" (kurgusal). "Composite character" | WRONG NUMBER. THEN A FRIENDSHIP. |
| 2–14 sn | Haftalar boyunca uzayan sohbet ekranı | WEEKS OF FRIENDLY CHAT |
| 14–26 sn | "Look how well my app is doing" + grafik ekran görüntüsü (kurgusal) | THEN: AN INVESTMENT APP |
| 26–36 sn | "A real wrong number says sorry and stops." | REAL WRONG NUMBERS STOP |

**VO:**
> Wrong number. Then a friendship.
> "Hi Susan, still on for golf?" Carol replied kindly: wrong person. The stranger apologized, then kept chatting, warm and easy, for weeks. Then the new friend mentioned an investment app that was doing great, and sent screenshots. The FTC says wrong-number texts often grow into friendly or romantic conversations that end in fake investments. A real wrong number says sorry and stops. So if a stranger's text turns into…

**Döngü:** "…turns into…" → "Wrong number. Then a friendship."
**Kaynak:** FTC – New FTC data show top text message scams of 2024; overall losses to text scams hit $470 million (April 2025)
