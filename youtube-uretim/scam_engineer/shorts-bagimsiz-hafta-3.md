## Short 9 — 24 Kas | GERÇEK Mİ SAHTE Mİ?: "Şüpheli Amazon siparişi" (~34 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Üç kutu (kargo kolisi çizimi, logo yok) sırayla açılıyor, her birinden bir mesaj çıkıyor | REAL OR FAKE? A SUSPICIOUS ORDER. |
| 2–14 sn | "An expensive phone was ordered on your account. Press 1 to cancel." | FAKE |
| 14–22 sn | Kendi uygulamanda, siparişlerin arasında bilmediğin bir kalem | CHECK IN YOUR OWN APP |
| 22–34 sn | "We refunded too much. Send back the difference." | FAKE |

**VO:**
> Real or fake? A suspicious order.
> A call says an expensive phone was ordered on your Amazon account. Press one to cancel. Fake. The FTC says scammers pose as Amazon this way, then ask for remote access or gift cards to give you a "refund." Second: an order you don't recognize, inside your own Amazon app. Real, and you can handle it right there. Third: "We refunded too much, send the difference back." Fake. Big shopping week, more fake calls. So, real or fake…

**Döngü:** "…real or fake…" → "Real or fake? A suspicious order."
**Kaynak:** FTC – Did you get a call or text about a suspicious purchase on Amazon? It's a scam (March 2024); FTC Data Spotlight – Amazon tops list of impersonated businesses

## Short 10 — 25 Kas | QUIZ: Fazla ödeyen alıcı (~35 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Bir çek (kurgusal) masaya kayıyor, üstünde fazla tutar daire içine alınıyor | THEY OVERPAID. SEND THE DIFFERENCE? |
| 2–12 sn | Şıklar | A) YES, IT CLEARED B) WAIT A DAY C) NO |
| 12–15 sn | Sayaç | 3… 2… 1… |
| 15–35 sn | "C" yeşil; "AVAILABLE ≠ GOOD" | C) AVAILABLE ISN'T CLEARED |

**VO:**
> They overpaid. Send the difference?
> A buyer sends a check for more than your price and asks you to return the extra. The money shows in your account. A: yes, the bank cleared it. B: wait a day, then send it. C: no. It's C. The FTC says banks must make deposited funds available within a day or two, but it can take weeks to uncover a fake check. When it bounces, you owe the bank the full amount. The overpayment is the giveaway. So when a stranger asks:

**Döngü:** "…a stranger asks:" → "They overpaid. Send the difference?"
**Kaynak:** FTC – How to spot, avoid, and report fake check scams; FTC Data Spotlight – Fake checks

## Short 11 — 27 Kas | KISA HİKÂYE: Sipariş edilmemiş yenileme (~36 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** E-posta kutusunda bir fatura açılıyor: "Plan renewed: $399" (kurgusal marka). "Composite character" | A $399 RENEWAL HE NEVER ORDERED. |
| 2–14 sn | "Call to cancel" numarası vurgulu | "CALL TO CANCEL" |
| 14–26 sn | Bilgisayar ekranında uzaktan erişim penceresi açılıyor | "I NEED REMOTE ACCESS" |
| 26–36 sn | "Refund too big, send the difference" | REAL REFUNDS DON'T NEED ACCESS |

**VO:**
> A $399 renewal he never ordered.
> The email said Bill's computer protection plan had renewed, with a number to call to cancel. A friendly agent offered a refund, but first needed remote access to Bill's computer. Then the "refund" looked too big, and Bill was asked to send the difference back. The FTC has warned about fake renewal emails exactly like this. Real companies don't need remote access to refund you. Check your card statement directly. It all started with…

**Döngü:** "…It all started with…" → "A $399 renewal he never ordered."
**Kaynak:** FTC – How to recognize a fake Geek Squad renewal scam (Oct 2022); FTC – Tech support scams

## Short 12 — 29 Kas | GERÇEK Mİ SAHTE Mİ?: Mükemmel kiralık ev (~33 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Kiralık ev ilanı (Pexels ev fotoğrafı, kurgusal fiyat) kayarak giriyor, fiyat yanıp sönüyor | REAL OR FAKE? THE PERFECT RENTAL. |
| 2–12 sn | "Owner overseas. Send deposit to hold it." | FAKE |
| 12–20 sn | Kapıda anahtar teslimi (eller, yüz yok) + kira sözleşmesi | MORE LIKELY REAL |
| 20–33 sn | "Deposit by gift card, wire, or crypto" | FAKE |

**VO:**
> Real or fake? The perfect rental.
> A great price, but the owner is overseas and wants a deposit before you see it. Fake. The FTC lists both as red flags. Second: a landlord who shows you inside and gives you a written lease. More likely real. Third: pay the deposit by gift card, wire, or crypto. Fake. Search the address online. If the same home is listed elsewhere with a different contact or price, walk away. Next listing you love? Real or fake…

**Döngü:** "…Real or fake…" → "Real or fake? The perfect rental."
**Kaynak:** FTC – Rental listing scams (consumer.ftc.gov/articles/rental-listing-scams)
