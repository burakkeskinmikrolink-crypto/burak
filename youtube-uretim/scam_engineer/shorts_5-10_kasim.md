## 5 Kasım: Spoofing | QUIZ (A/B/C)
**Başlık:** Your bank's real number is calling. Is it your bank?
**Görsel:** Dikey ekranda gelen arama: "First National Bank – Fraud Dept." (kurgusal banka), altında gerçek görünen bir numara. Şıklar sırayla beliriyor, 3 sn düşünme sayacı.

| Zaman | Ekran | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Gelen arama ekranı | Your phone shows your bank's real number. The caller says there's fraud on your account. |
| 4–14 sn | Şıklar A/B/C | What proves it's really your bank? A) The number matches the one on your card. B) They know your name and last four digits. C) Nothing on this call proves it. |
| 14–17 sn | Sayaç 3-2-1 | *(sessizlik + tik sesi)* |
| 17–32 sn | "C" yeşil yanıyor | It's C. Caller ID can be faked. That's called spoofing. And your name and last four digits are often already leaked. The only proof is a call you start: hang up, and dial the number on the back of your card. |
| 32–35 sn | Kanal logosu | I'm a telecom engineer. Full breakdown on the channel. |

## 6 Kasım: SIM swap | KISA HİKÂYE
**Başlık:** Her phone said "SOS only." Her bank account was next.
**Görsel:** POV: Telefon ekranının sağ üst köşesinde sinyal çubukları kayboluyor, "SOS" beliriyor. Ardından e-posta bildirimleri.

**VO (≈40 sn):**
> Linda's phone was fine at lunch. By two o'clock, it just said "SOS." No calls. No texts. She figured it was a network glitch.
> It wasn't. Someone had convinced her carrier to move her phone number to their SIM card. Now every security code her bank texted went to them, not her.
> Here's the part that matters. Since FCC rules adopted in 2023, carriers must notify you right away when someone requests a SIM change or a number transfer, and offer a free account lock.
> So if your phone suddenly loses service for no reason, call your carrier from another phone. Fast. And turn on that lock today.

*(Ekranda: "Composite character")*

## 7 Kasım: Spoofing | GERÇEK Mİ SAHTE Mİ?
**Başlık:** Real or fake? 3 things a "bank" caller says
**Görsel:** Her ifade bir konuşma balonunda. Altında "REAL / FAKE" butonları, 2 sn bekleyip cevap.

| # | Ekrandaki ifade | Cevap | VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "We'll text you a code. Read it back so we can verify you." | **FAKE** | Fake. That code is the key to your account. Your bank uses it to verify *you*, not the other way round. Never read it to a caller. |
| 2 | "Please hang up and call the number on your card." | **REAL** | Real. A genuine bank is always fine with you calling back. |
| 3 | "Your caller ID shows our number, so you know it's us." | **FAKE** | Fake. Caller ID can be spoofed. A matching number proves nothing. |

**Kapanış VO:** One rule beats all three: you make the call. They don't.

## 8 Kasım: SIM swap | QUIZ (A/B/C)
**Başlık:** The best defense against SIM swap? (Most people pick wrong)
**Görsel:** Üç kart açılıyor: A, B, C.

**VO (≈35 sn):**
> Quiz. Which single step makes a SIM swap least useful to a scammer?
> A) A stronger password on your email.
> B) Moving your important accounts from text-message codes to an authenticator app or security key, where available.
> C) Turning your phone off at night.
> *(3 sn bekleme)*
> It's B. A SIM swap steals your phone number, and with it, your text-message codes. Codes from an authenticator app or a security key live on your device, not your number. Add a carrier account lock, and you've closed both doors.

## 9 Kasım: Spoofing | KISA HİKÂYE
**Başlık:** The call came from his bank. The voice didn't.
**Görsel:** Mutfak masası, sabit telefon/akıllı telefon, kahve fincanı. Yüz yok.

**VO (≈40 sn):**
> Tom's phone rang. The screen said his bank's name, with the same number that's printed on his card. A polite woman said someone was trying to buy a laptop with his debit card. "I'll send you a code to block it," she said.
> The code arrived. Tom started reading it out… then stopped. He remembered one thing: real banks don't ask you to read codes back.
> He hung up, flipped his card over, and called the number on the back. His bank had never called him.
> The number was real. The caller wasn't. That's spoofing.

*(Ekranda: "Composite character")*

## 10 Kasım: SIM swap | GERÇEK Mİ SAHTE Mİ?
**Başlık:** Real or fake? SIM swap warning signs
**Görsel:** Telefon bildirimleri tek tek düşüyor. Her birinin altında "REAL SIGN / NOT A SIGN".

| # | Ekrandaki bildirim/durum | Cevap | VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Telefonun aniden "SOS only / No service" gösteriyor ve yakınındakilerin telefonu çalışıyor | **REAL SIGN** | Real warning sign. If everyone else has signal and you suddenly don't, call your carrier from another phone. |
| 2 | Operatörden SMS/e-posta: "A SIM change was requested on your account." Sen istemedin | **REAL SIGN** | Real, and important. Carriers now have to alert you to SIM change requests. If it wasn't you, act now. |
| 3 | "Your phone has been SIM-swapped! Click here to fix it." | **FAKE** | Fake. That's a phishing text pretending to be a warning. Never fix anything through a link. Call your carrier directly. |

**Kapanış VO:** I'm a telecom engineer. Full SIM swap explainer on the channel.
