## 5 Kasım: Spoofing | QUIZ (A/B/C)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Telefon çalıyor, ekranda kurgusal "First National Bank – Fraud Dept." + gerçek görünen numara | YOUR BANK'S NUMBER. YOUR BANK? |
| 2–12 sn | Şıklar tek tek | A) NUMBER MATCHES B) KNOWS YOUR NAME C) NOTHING |
| 12–15 sn | Sayaç | 3… 2… 1… |
| 15–32 sn | "C" yeşil | C) HANG UP, DIAL THE CARD |

**VO:**
> Your bank's number. Your bank?
> What proves it's really them? A: the number matches the one on your card. B: they know your name and the last four digits. C: nothing on this call proves it.
> It's C. Caller ID can be faked; that's spoofing. Your name and digits are often already leaked. The only proof is a call you start, from the number on your card. So next time your screen says…

**Döngü:** "…your screen says…" → "Your bank's number. Your bank?"

## 6 Kasım: SIM swap | KISA HİKÂYE

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Sinyal çubukları tek tek sönüyor, "SOS" beliriyor. Köşede "Composite character" | HER PHONE SAID SOS. |
| 2–14 sn | Bankadan gelen kod bildirimi başka bir telefona gidiyor | HER CODES WENT TO SOMEONE ELSE |
| 14–26 sn | Operatör bildirimi: "SIM change requested" | CARRIERS MUST ALERT YOU |
| 26–36 sn | "Account lock: ON" anahtarı | TURN ON THE FREE LOCK |

**VO:**
> Her phone said SOS.
> At lunch, Linda's phone was fine. By two o'clock, no calls, no texts. Someone had convinced her carrier to move her number to their SIM card, so her bank's security codes went to them.
> Under FCC rules adopted in 2023, carriers must alert you to SIM change requests and offer a free account lock. If your service vanishes for no reason, call your carrier from another phone, fast. Because it all started the moment…

**Döngü:** "…the moment…" → "Her phone said SOS."

## 7 Kasım: Spoofing | GERÇEK Mİ SAHTE Mİ?

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Üç konuşma balonu sırayla patlayarak açılıyor | REAL OR FAKE? THREE BANK LINES. |
| 2–12 sn | "Read the code back so we can verify you." | FAKE |
| 12–20 sn | "Please hang up and call the number on your card." | REAL |
| 20–32 sn | "Caller ID shows our number, so it's us." | FAKE |

**VO:**
> Real or fake? Three bank lines.
> "We'll text you a code. Read it back so we can verify you." Fake. That code is your key. Never read it to a caller.
> "Please hang up and call the number on your card." Real. A genuine bank is always fine with that.
> "Caller ID shows our number, so you know it's us." Fake. Caller ID can be spoofed. Ready for the next caller? Real or fake…

**Döngü:** "…Real or fake…" → "Real or fake? Three bank lines."

## 8 Kasım: SIM swap | QUIZ (A/B/C)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Bir SIM kartı tepsiden fırlayıp başka bir telefona kayıyor | BEST DEFENSE AGAINST SIM SWAP? |
| 2–12 sn | Şıklar | A) PASSWORD B) AUTHENTICATOR APP C) PHONE OFF |
| 12–15 sn | Sayaç | 3… 2… 1… |
| 15–32 sn | "B" yeşil + kilit ikonu | B) + CARRIER ACCOUNT LOCK |

**VO:**
> Best defense against SIM swap?
> A: a stronger email password. B: moving important accounts from text-message codes to an authenticator app or security key, where available. C: turning your phone off at night.
> It's B. A SIM swap steals your number, and your text codes with it. App and key codes live on your device, not your number. Add your carrier's account lock, and you've closed both doors. So, one more time — what's the…

**Döngü:** "…what's the…" → "Best defense against SIM swap?"

## 9 Kasım: Spoofing | KISA HİKÂYE

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Telefon ekranında gelen arama, bankanın numarası yanıp sönüyor. Köşede "Composite character" | THE NUMBER WAS REAL. HE WASN'T. |
| 2–14 sn | Kod SMS'i geliyor, parmak duruyor | "READ ME THE CODE" |
| 14–26 sn | Kart çevriliyor, arkadaki numara aranıyor | HE CALLED THE CARD NUMBER |
| 26–36 sn | Gelen arama ekranına geri dönüş | THE BANK NEVER CALLED |

**VO:**
> The number was real. He wasn't.
> Tom's phone showed his bank's name and the exact number printed on his card. A polite voice said someone was buying a laptop with his debit card, and sent a code "to block it."
> Tom started reading the code… then stopped. Real banks don't ask for codes back. He hung up and called the number on his card. His bank had never called. Spoofing, in one sentence:

**Döngü:** "…in one sentence:" → "The number was real. He wasn't."

## 10 Kasım: SIM swap | GERÇEK Mİ SAHTE Mİ?

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Bildirimler ekrana tek tek düşüyor | REAL WARNING OR FAKE? |
| 2–12 sn | "SOS only", çevredeki telefonlarda sinyal var | REAL WARNING SIGN |
| 12–22 sn | Operatör: "A SIM change was requested." Sen istemedin | REAL — ACT NOW |
| 22–32 sn | "Your phone was SIM-swapped! Click to fix." | FAKE |

**VO:**
> Real warning or fake?
> Your phone suddenly says "SOS only," while everyone around you has signal. Real warning sign. Call your carrier from another phone.
> A message from your carrier: "A SIM change was requested on your account." If it wasn't you, it's real, and urgent.
> "Your phone was SIM-swapped! Click here to fix it." Fake. Never fix anything through a link. Next notification, same question: real warning…

**Döngü:** "…real warning…" → "Real warning or fake?"
