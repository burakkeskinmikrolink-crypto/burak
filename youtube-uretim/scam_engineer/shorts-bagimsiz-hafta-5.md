## Short 17 — 8 Ara | KISA HİKÂYE: Torununun sesi (~35 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Ses dalgası formu bir telefondan yükseliyor, tanıdık bir siluete dönüşüyor. "Composite character" | IT SOUNDED EXACTLY LIKE HER GRANDSON. |
| 2–14 sn | "Grandma, I'm in trouble. Don't tell Mom." | "DON'T TELL MOM" |
| 14–24 sn | Mutfak masasında kâğıda yazılmış "CODE WORD?" (yüz yok) | SHE ASKED FOR THE CODE WORD |
| 24–35 sn | "Hang up. Call back on a number you know." | AGREE ON ONE THIS HOLIDAY |

**VO:**
> It sounded exactly like her grandson.
> "Grandma, I'm in trouble. Please don't tell Mom." Margaret almost reached for her purse. Then she asked the question the family had agreed on months earlier: their code word. Silence. The line went dead. The FTC warns that scammers can use AI to clone a voice from short clips posted online. Hang up and call the person on a number you know. And agree on a family code word over the holidays. Even when…

**Döngü:** "…Even when…" → "It sounded exactly like her grandson."
**Kaynak:** FTC – Scammers use AI to enhance their family emergency schemes (March 2023)

## Short 18 — 9 Ara | GERÇEK Mİ SAHTE Mİ?: Öğrenci kredisi affı (~33 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Mezuniyet kepi havaya atılıyor, düşerken bir fiyat etiketine dönüşüyor | REAL OR FAKE? STUDENT LOAN FORGIVENESS. |
| 2–12 sn | "Pay a $499 fee and we'll get your loans forgiven." (kurgusal) | FAKE |
| 12–22 sn | Resmi site adresi düz metin: StudentAid.gov | FREE AND REAL |
| 22–33 sn | "What's your FSA ID? We'll process it." | FAKE |

**VO:**
> Real or fake? Student loan forgiveness.
> "Pay a 499 dollar fee and we'll get your loans forgiven." Fake. The FTC says it's illegal for companies to charge you before they help reduce your student loan debt. Second: help at StudentAid.gov, or from your loan servicer. Real, and free. Third: a caller asking for your FSA ID to "process" it. Fake. Never share it. Nothing a company does for a fee can't be done by you, for free. So, real or fake…

**Döngü:** "…real or fake…" → "Real or fake? Student loan forgiveness."
**Kaynak:** FTC – Student loan debt relief scams (consumer.ftc.gov); FTC press release – FTC stops scammers charged with preying on students seeking debt relief (Aug 2023)

**Döngü:** "…a message says:" → "You won! Just pay the fees?"

## Short 20 — 13 Ara | KISA HİKÂYE: Hiç görüntülü konuşamadı (~35 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Görüntülü arama ekranı: "Connection lost" tekrar tekrar. "Composite character" | HE NEVER COULD VIDEO CHAT. |
| 2–14 sn | Aylar süren mesajlaşma, "working overseas" | ALWAYS A BAD CONNECTION |
| 14–24 sn | "I need money for a ticket home." | THEN: A REQUEST FOR MONEY |
| 24–35 sn | Bir kız ve anne silueti (yüz yok) bilgisayarda görsel arama yapıyor | HIS PHOTOS WERE SOMEONE ELSE'S |

**VO:**
> He never could video chat.
> Helen met Mark online. He was working overseas, he said, and calls always had a bad connection. After months of messages, he needed money for a ticket home. The FTC says romance scammers always have a reason they can't meet or video chat, and sooner or later they ask for money. Helen talked to her daughter first. Together they searched his photos and found them on someone else's profile. Now it made sense, because…

**Döngü:** "…because…" → "He never could video chat."
**Kaynak:** FTC – What you need to know about romance scams (consumer.ftc.gov)
