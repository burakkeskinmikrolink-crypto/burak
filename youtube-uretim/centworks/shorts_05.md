## Short 1: "Fed mortgage faizini belirlemez" (efsane kırıcı)
- **Süre:** ~35 sn
- **Hook:** THE FED DOESN'T SET YOUR MORTGAGE RATE

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Kırmızı damga "MYTH?" + ev ikonu | "THE FED SETS MORTGAGE RATES" |
| 4–12 sn | Fed binası (Wikimedia Commons) | FED SETS: OVERNIGHT BANK RATE |
| 12–22 sn | Grafik: 10 yıllık tahvil ve mortgage çizgileri birlikte hareket ediyor | MORTGAGES FOLLOW LONG-TERM BONDS |
| 22–35 sn | Freddie Mac rakamları | OCT 2026: 7.40% PEAK |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Myth: the Fed sets your mortgage rate. It doesn't.
> The Fed sets one short-term rate: what banks charge each other overnight.
> Mortgage rates mostly follow long-term bond yields, which move on what investors expect inflation and the Fed to do over years.
> So when markets started expecting more hikes this fall, mortgage rates rose. Freddie Mac's thirty-year average hit seven point four percent in early October.
> The Fed matters to your mortgage. Just indirectly.

**Açıklama:** Fed mortgage faizini doğrudan belirlemez. Peki o zaman neden yükseldi?

## Short 2: "15 dolar mı, 1.270 dolar mı?" (rakam karşılaştırma)
- **Süre:** ~30 sn
- **Hook:** $15 vs $1,270

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Bölünmüş ekran: "$15" ve "$1,270" | WHICH ONE SHOULD WORRY YOU? |
| 4–12 sn | Sol taraf büyüyor | +0.25% FED HIKE ON $6,000 ≈ $15/YEAR |
| 12–22 sn | Sağ taraf büyüyor | ~21% APR ON $6,000 ≈ $1,270/YEAR |
| 22–30 sn | Kart kesiliyor animasyonu | THE LEVEL IS THE PROBLEM |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Fifteen dollars or twelve hundred and seventy? Here's the credit card math nobody shows you.
> A quarter-point Fed hike on a six-thousand-dollar balance adds about fifteen dollars a year in interest.
> But at an average card rate of around twenty-one percent, that same balance already costs about twelve hundred and seventy dollars a year.
> The hike isn't the problem. The balance is. Paying it down helps whether the Fed raises, holds or cuts.

## Short 3: "Ya devam ederse?" (senaryo kartları)
- **Süre:** ~40 sn
- **Hook:** IF THE FED HIKES AGAIN…

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | "IF THE FED HIKES AGAIN…" | 4 THINGS HAPPEN |
| 4–12 sn | Ev ikonu | FIXED MORTGAGE: NO CHANGE |
| 12–20 sn | Kart ikonu | CREDIT CARD APR: UP |
| 20–28 sn | Kumbara | SAVINGS: UP, IF YOU SHOP AROUND |
| 28–40 sn | Grafik aşağı eğiliyor | ECONOMY: SLOWS (ON PURPOSE) |

**VO (tam metin):**
> If the Fed raises rates again, here's what happens.
> Your fixed-rate mortgage? Nothing. That's the point of fixed.
> Your credit card? The rate goes up, usually within a billing cycle or two.
> Your savings? High-yield accounts tend to pay more, but only if your bank passes it on.
> And the economy slows down. That's not a side effect. It's the goal: cooling inflation.
> Whatever happens next, paying down variable-rate debt is the move that works in every scenario. The full what-if is on Centworks.

**Açıklama:** Fed faizi artırmaya devam ederse mortgage, kart ve birikim ne olur?
