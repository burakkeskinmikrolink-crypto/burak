## Short 1: "Bölünmüş ekran hikâye"
- **Süre:** ~35 sn
- **Hook:** SAME GROCERIES. ONLY ONE GOT PAID.

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Bölünmüş ekran: solda kart, sağda nakit, aynı market poşeti | SAME $100 · SAME BAGS |
| 4–10 sn | Sol tarafta "+$2" puan | LEFT: +$2 IN REWARDS |
| 10–16 sn | Sağ taraf "$0" | RIGHT: $0 |
| 16–26 sn | Mağazadan karta giden ücret oku → raf fiyat etiketi | THE SWIPE FEE IS IN THE SHELF PRICE |
| 26–35 sn | "$149 / year" | BOSTON FED ESTIMATE (2010) |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Same groceries. Same hundred-dollar total.
> Left pays with a rewards card and gets two dollars back. Right pays cash and gets nothing.
> But the store paid a swipe fee on the card, and that cost is built into the shelf price for both of them.
> A Boston Fed study estimated that, on average, cash-paying households were transferring about one hundred and forty-nine dollars a year to card users.
> Rewards aren't free. They're shared.

**Açıklama:** Kredi kartı ödülleri, herkesin ödediği raf fiyatının içinden finanse ediliyor.
**Hashtag:** #creditcards #rewards #swipefees #personalfinance #centworks
**Kaynak:** Boston Fed (Schuh, Shy, Stavins 2010), NPR Planet Money

## Short 2: "Bu yıl ne değişiyor?" (haber formatı)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |

| 0–4 sn | Kasada kurgusal tabela "Premium cards not accepted" | COMING TO A STORE NEAR YOU? |

| 22–30 sn | Soru işareti | FINAL DECISION PENDING |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Your rewards card might not be welcome everywhere soon.
> A revised Visa and Mastercard settlement got preliminary approval from a federal judge in June 2026.
> If it's finalized, merchants could choose to accept standard cards but decline premium rewards cards, and add surcharges more easily.
> Will stores actually do it? Nobody knows yet. Many may worry about losing customers. But the "we take every card" era may be ending.

**Açıklama:** Visa ve Mastercard ile işyerleri arasındaki uzlaşma, premium kartların kabulünü değiştirebilir.

## Short 3: "Kazanan mı kaybeden mi? 3 soru" (kendini test et)
- **Süre:** ~40 sn
- **Hook:** ARE YOU WINNING THE REWARDS GAME?

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Skor tablosu | 3 QUESTIONS |
| 4–12 sn | "1" + onay kutusu | PAY IN FULL EVERY MONTH? |
| 12–20 sn | "2" + onay kutusu | NO EXTRA SPENDING FOR POINTS? |
| 20–28 sn | "3" + onay kutusu | REWARDS > ANNUAL FEE? |
| 28–40 sn | Yeşil "3 × YES" / kırmızı "ANY NO" | INTEREST CAN WIPE OUT REWARDS |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Are you winning or losing the credit card rewards game? Three questions.
> One: do you pay your full balance every month?
> Two: do you avoid spending more just to earn points?
> Three: do your rewards actually beat any annual fee?
> Three yeses? You're likely on the winning side. Any no, especially the first one? Research suggests interest charges can wipe out your rewards and then some.
> That's not advice. It's math.

**Açıklama:** Kredi kartı ödüllerinde kazanan tarafta mısın? Üç soruluk hızlı test.
**Hashtag:** #creditcards #rewards #moneytips #debt #centworks
**Kaynak:** Agarwal, Presbitero, Silva, Wix, "Who Pays for Your Rewards?"
