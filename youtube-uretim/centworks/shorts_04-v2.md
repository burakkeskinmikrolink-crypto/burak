**VO:**
> Winning the rewards game?
> Three questions. Do you pay your full balance every month? Do you avoid spending more just to earn points? Do your rewards beat any annual fee?
> Three yeses, and you're likely on the winning side. Any no — especially the first — and research suggests interest can wipe out your rewards and more. That's not advice. It's math. So, honestly… are you…
