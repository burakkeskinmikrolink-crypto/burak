**VO:**
> Gift cards expire in a year?
> Usually not. Since 2010, a federal law, the Credit CARD Act, has said money on most gift cards can't expire for at least five years from when it was issued or last loaded. Inactivity fees are only allowed after twelve months without use, at most once a month, and they must be printed on the card or packaging. Some states protect you even more.
