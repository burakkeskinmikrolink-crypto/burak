## Short 1: Efsane kırıcı (~33 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Ev ikonu üstüne kırmızı "MYTH?" damgası düşüyor | THE FED SETS MORTGAGE RATES? |
| 2–10 sn | Fed binası (Wikimedia Commons) | FED SETS: OVERNIGHT BANK RATE |
| 10–22 sn | 10 yıllık tahvil ve mortgage çizgileri birlikte hareket ediyor | MORTGAGES FOLLOW LONG-TERM BONDS |
| 22–33 sn | "7.40%" | OCT 2026 PEAK |

**VO:**
> The Fed sets mortgage rates?
> Not directly. The Fed sets one short-term rate: what banks charge each other overnight.
> Mortgage rates mostly follow long-term bond yields, which move on what investors expect over years. When markets expected more hikes this fall, Freddie Mac's thirty-year average reached seven point four percent. The Fed sets the mood, not your rate. So next time someone tells you…

| 2–10 sn | Sol taraf | +0.25% HIKE ≈ $15/YR |
| 10–20 sn | Sağ taraf | ~21% APR ≈ $1,270/YR |
| 20–30 sn | Kart kesiliyor | THE BALANCE IS THE PROBLEM |

**VO:**
> $15 or $1,270?
> A quarter-point Fed hike on a six-thousand-dollar card balance adds about fifteen dollars a year in interest.
> But at an average card rate around twenty-one percent, that balance already costs about twelve hundred and seventy a year. The hike isn't the problem. The balance is. Paying it down helps whether the Fed raises, holds or cuts. So which number should worry you —

## Short 3: Üç dal (~36 sn)

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 sn | **Hareketli:** Üç dallı yol animasyonu, dallar sırayla parlıyor | HIKE, HOLD, OR CUT? |
| 2–12 sn | "HIKE" dalı | CARD APR UP |
| 12–22 sn | "HOLD" dalı | SAVINGS: SHOP AROUND |
| 22–30 sn | "CUT" dalı | BORROWING CHEAPER, SLOWLY |
| 30–36 sn | Ortak madde parlıyor | PAY DOWN VARIABLE DEBT |

**VO:**
> Hike, hold, or cut?
> If the Fed hikes, credit card rates rise within a billing cycle or two. If it holds, high-yield savings keep paying more, if you shop around. If it cuts, borrowing gets cheaper, slowly.
> And your fixed-rate mortgage? It doesn't move in any branch. In every branch, one move helps: paying down variable-rate debt. Because nobody knows which way it goes next. Hike…
