| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |

**VO:**
> Check the box on your statement.
> Every credit card statement has a warning box. By law, it shows how long it would take to pay off your balance if you only made minimum payments, and how much you'd pay in total. It also shows what you'd need to pay each month to clear it in three years. Those numbers assume you buy nothing new. Most people never read it, but it might be the most honest number your card company sends. So tonight, open the app and…

Pay the full statement balance by the due date, and new purchases usually cost zero interest. > 35% of your credit score.

**VO:**
> Why doesn't the Fed target zero?
> Since 2012, the Federal Reserve has aimed for 2% inflation over the longer run. Not zero. A little inflation gives the economy a buffer against deflation, when prices fall and people delay spending, which can feed on itself. It also leaves the Fed more room to cut interest rates in a downturn. And official measures may overstate inflation slightly. Two percent is a target, not a promise.
