## Short 1: "Hesapla" mini quiz
- **Süre:** ~30 sn
- **Hook:** $7.48 IN CASH. WHAT DO YOU PAY?

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Market fişi, "TOTAL $7.48" | $7.48 · CASH · NO PENNIES |
| 4–10 sn | Şıklar | A) $7.45 B) $7.50 C) $7.48 |
| 10–13 sn | Sayaç | 3… 2… 1… |
| 13–24 sn | Sayı doğrusu: 1–2 ↓, 3–4 → 5, 6–7 → 5, 8–9 ↑ | B) $7.50 |
| 24–30 sn | Kart terminali "$7.48" | CARD? STILL $7.48 |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Pop quiz. Your total is seven forty-eight, and you're paying cash. With no pennies, what do you pay?
> It's B: seven fifty. Eight rounds up to the next ten.
> Here's the whole rule: one and two round down, three and four go to five, six and seven go to five, eight and nine go up.
> And if you pay by card? Still seven forty-eight. Exactly.
> Follow Centworks for how money really works.

**Açıklama:** Kuruş olmadan nakitte yuvarlama nasıl yapılıyor? Kart ödemeleri yuvarlanmıyor.

## Short 2: "Bir rakam"
- **Süre:** ~35 sn
- **Hook:** 3.69¢

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Dev "3.69¢" | 3.69¢ |
| 4–12 sn | Kuruş (ABD Darphanesi kamu görseli) | COST TO MAKE ONE PENNY |
| 12–20 sn | Philadelphia Darphanesi (Wikimedia Commons) | NOV 2025: LAST CIRCULATING PENNY |
| 20–30 sn | 5 sent görseli + "13.78¢" | THE NICKEL: ~13.8¢ TO MAKE |
| 30–35 sn | Kanal logosu | NEXT: A CHEAPER NICKEL? |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Three point six nine cents. That's what it cost the U.S. Mint to make one penny. A coin worth one cent.
> So in November 2025, America made its last circulating penny.
> And the nickel? About thirteen point eight cents to make a five-cent coin.
> Congress has now passed a law that lets the Mint try a cheaper nickel, if testing shows vending machines can handle it.
> Your coins are getting a makeover, because they cost more than they're worth.

## Short 3: "Efsane mi gerçek mi?"
- **Süre:** ~35 sn
- **Hook:** MYTH: ROUNDING IS A SECRET TAX

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | Kırmızı damga "MYTH?" | "ROUNDING IS A SECRET TAX" |
| 4–14 sn | Richmond Fed binası (Wikimedia Commons) | RICHMOND FED ESTIMATE |
| 14–22 sn | Dev "$6 MILLION / YEAR" | TOTAL. WHOLE COUNTRY. |
| 22–30 sn | Yukarı/aşağı oklar birbirini götürüyor | UPS AND DOWNS CANCEL OUT |
| 30–35 sn | Nakit vs kart ikonu | THE REAL CHANGE: 2 PRICES |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Myth: penny rounding is a secret tax on shoppers.
> Here's what the Richmond Fed found. Rounding cash totals to the nearest nickel costs consumers about six million dollars a year in total. That's the whole country, not per person.
> Why so little? The ups and downs mostly cancel out, and card payments aren't rounded at all.
> The real change isn't your wallet. It's that cash and card can now have two different prices.

**Açıklama:** Yuvarlama gizli bir vergi mi? Richmond Fed'in hesabı.
**Hashtag:** #penny #richmondfed #economics #mythbusting #centworks
