## Short 1: "Faizsizse nasıl para kazanıyorlar?"
- **Süre:** ~35 sn
- **Hook:** 0% INTEREST. SO WHO PAYS?

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | "Pay in 4 · 0% interest" butonu (kurgusal) | 0% INTEREST. SO WHO PAYS? |
| 4–14 sn | Animasyon: BNPL şirketi mağazaya peşin ödüyor, eksi bir ücret | 1. THE STORE PAYS A FEE |
| 14–22 sn | Takvim, kırmızı bir gün | 2. SOME CHARGE LATE FEES |
| 22–30 sn | "6–24 months" etiketi | 3. LONGER PLANS CAN CHARGE INTEREST |
| 30–35 sn | Kanal logosu | ONE NAME. MANY PRODUCTS. |

**VO (tam metin):**
> If "pay in four" is interest-free, how do these companies make money?
> One: the store pays. The buy-now-pay-later company pays the merchant up front, minus a fee, because shoppers who see "four easy payments" tend to buy more.
> Two: some providers charge late fees if you miss a payment.
> Three: longer plans, six to twenty-four months, often charge interest.
> "Buy now, pay later" isn't one product. Only some of them are free.

**Açıklama:** BNPL şirketleri "faizsiz" taksitle nasıl para kazanıyor?

## Short 2: "%4,1 mi, %47 mi?" (veri bilmecesi)
- **Süre:** ~35 sn
- **Hook:** 4.1% OR 47%? BOTH ARE TRUE.

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | İki dev rakam | 4.1% vs 47% |
| 4–12 sn | "4.1%" altında "CFPB · loans · 2023" | LOANS HIT WITH A LATE FEE |
| 12–20 sn | "47%" altında "LendingTree survey · people · 2026" | USERS WHO SAY THEY PAID LATE |
| 20–35 sn | Bir kişi, altı kredi ikonu, biri kırmızı | 6 LOANS, 1 LATE = A LATE PAYER |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Four point one percent or forty-seven percent: how often do buy-now-pay-later users pay late? Both numbers are real.
> The CFPB found that in 2023, four point one percent of pay-in-four loans were hit with a late fee.
> A 2026 LendingTree survey found forty-seven percent of users said they'd paid late in the past year.
> The difference? One counts loans. The other counts people. Take out six loans, pay one late, and most of your loans were fine. But you still paid late.

**Açıklama:** BNPL gecikme oranları neden bu kadar farklı görünüyor? Kredi sayısı ile kişi sayısı arasındaki fark.
**Hashtag:** #bnpl #statistics #personalfinance #cfpb #centworks
**Kaynak:** CFPB BNPL Market Report (Aralık 2025), LendingTree BNPL Tracker (2026)

## Short 3: "Tek kural" (POV, kasada)
- **Süre:** ~30 sn
- **Hook:** ONE QUESTION BEFORE YOU TAP

| Zaman | Görsel | Ekran yazısı |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sn | POV: telefonda iki buton, "Pay in 4" ve "Card" | BLACK FRIDAY CHECKOUT |
| 4–14 sn | Parmak butonların üstünde duruyor | "CAN I PAY ALL OF IT TODAY?" |
| 14–22 sn | "YES" → kart, "paid in full" | YES → CARD, PAID IN FULL |
| 22–30 sn | "NO" → sepetten ürün çıkıyor | NO → NEITHER MAKES IT AFFORDABLE |

**VO (tam metin):**
> Before you tap "pay in four" or "pay with card" this Black Friday, ask one question: could I pay for all of this today?
> If yes, a credit card paid in full usually wins: no interest, purchase protection, maybe rewards.
> If you just need a couple of weeks, one pay-in-four plan, paid on time, can cost nothing.
> If the answer is no, neither button makes it affordable. They just change when the bill arrives.

**Açıklama:** BNPL mı kredi kartı mı? Black Friday'de karar verdiren tek soru.
**Hashtag:** #blackfriday #bnpl #creditcard #budgeting #centworks
**Kaynak:** FTC (Fair Credit Billing Act), ana videonun örnek hesabı
